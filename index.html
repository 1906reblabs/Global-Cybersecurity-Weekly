About This Publication

Global Cybersecurity Weekly is an independent, open-source threat intelligence digest compiled from open-source intelligence (OSINT), vendor threat research, government advisories, and industry reporting.

Each issue covers the most significant cybersecurity events of the preceding seven days, with analysis of strategic implications and actionable recommendations for security leaders, practitioners, and researchers.
